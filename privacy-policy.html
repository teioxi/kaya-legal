Privacy Policy

Kaya · Last updated October 25, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Kaya (the "App") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. It applies whether you use the App as a guest or with an account.

In short

  • We use your account and planner information to run the App, and our providers handle technical data.
  • Guest planner data stays on your device. Google ML Kit still handles technical diagnostic and usage data when you use registration import.
  • With an account, your planner is backed up to a private area that only you can read.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with other students or universities.
  • You can erase your device data or delete your account and cloud data from Profile.

1. Who we are

The App is provided by Carl, an independent developer, who is the "controller" of your personal information under data protection law. You can contact us at carlsteven0921@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We process the information described below to provide the App.

1. Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, and your password (handled by Firebase Authentication and never visible to us). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and display name.
  • Student profile: your name, university or school name, term system (semester, trimester or quarter), current term, academic year, grading scale, and the study goals you choose.
  • Planner information: your courses (name, code, instructor, room, colour, units and grade), your weekly class schedule, and your tasks (title, notes, due date and time, priority, whether it is done).

2. Information created by the App

  • Sync information: an internal ID and "last changed" time for each item, used to keep your devices consistent.
  • In-app notification history: a short list of update messages, and which notifications you have read or dismissed.
  • Focus timer log: when and for how long you studied, and which course you chose. It stays on your device.
  • Reminder and appearance choices (which reminders you want, light or dark theme), stored on your device.
  • Settings and sign-in state stored on your device.

3. Technical information handled by our providers

  • When you use sign-in or cloud backup, Google Firebase may process technical information such as your IP address, device and app identifiers, and diagnostic data, as part of running those services and keeping them secure.
  • When you use registration import, Google ML Kit collects technical diagnostic and usage data, including device and app information, a per-installation identifier, feature and API usage, performance and errors, and input/output formats and sizes. It may send this metadata to Google over an encrypted connection, including when you use the App as a guest. Text recognition runs on your device; the selected image and recognized text are not sent to Google for recognition.

Google ML Kit data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

Optional registration import: you can voluntarily select a PDF or image through the Android file picker. The App reads only the file you select, makes a private temporary copy on your device, and uses bundled Google ML Kit text recognition on the device to suggest course and schedule details. You can review and edit these suggestions before saving them.

We do not collect your precise location or contacts, or access your microphone or camera. We access photos and files only when you choose a document for import, without broad photo-library or storage permissions. The App has no advertising. Google ML Kit collects technical diagnostics and usage analytics as described above.

3. Where your information is stored

  • On your device: your planner information and settings are stored in a local database and local preferences on your phone. This is what makes the App work offline.
  • In the cloud (accounts only): if you sign in, your student profile and planner information are copied to Google Cloud Firestore, in a private area labelled with your account ID. Security rules allow only you, when signed in, to read or write that area.
  • Sign-in information is held by Firebase Authentication, which may process it on Google's servers in different countries.

Guest planner information is stored separately on this device. Signing in opens a separate account planner and does not automatically upload or merge guest information. Returning to guest mode restores the guest planner. Signing out keeps the account planner in a separate private local cache, which is opened again only for that signed-in account.

Unfinished planner forms, setup and profile edits, and registration-review suggestions are saved as local drafts so you can reopen the same form later. Drafts are separate for each account and guest mode, are not uploaded as drafts, and never contain passwords or original registration images. Raw extracted document text is not retained in drafts. Drafts are removed when you save or discard them, delete the owning account, erase the guest planner, or clear the app storage.

Registration documents and photos are processed on your device. The App removes its private temporary copy after normal completion or a handled error. If the app process stops before cleanup, that copy may remain in the private app cache; later app starts attempt to remove interrupted import copies once they are at least 24 hours old. The App does not upload the original document, photo or temporary copy to our Firebase services. The original file in the location you selected is left unchanged. Only the course and schedule details you approve and save become planner data: they are stored locally and, if you are signed in, can sync to your Firebase account.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • create and secure your account, and let you sign in;
  • show your schedule, tasks, courses and grade averages;
  • back up and sync your planner between your devices, and restore it if you change phones;
  • create in-app notifications and a once-a-day pop-up about deadlines and classes;
  • send reminders to your phone about classes and deadlines, if you turn them on;
  • show study statistics (focus time, finished tasks) to you on your device;
  • keep the App safe, prevent abuse, and fix problems;
  • meet legal obligations.

If you choose to import a registration document, we use its text on your device to suggest courses and class times for your review. Text recognition can make mistakes; check the suggested details against your school document before saving.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal effects on you.

5. Legal bases for using your information

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example, under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 or the EU General Data Protection Regulation), we rely on:

  • your consent, which you give when you create an account or enter information, and which you can withdraw by deleting your data or account;
  • performing our service to you, which means running the features you asked for;
  • our legitimate interests in keeping the App secure and working properly;
  • compliance with legal obligations.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with other students, universities, schools or advertisers.

The App uses the following providers to run its features:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for sign-in and cloud backup;
  • Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition and related technical diagnostics and usage analytics;
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google;
  • Google Play, which distributes the App and may give us basic technical reports.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the public, or the service.

7. How long we keep your information

  • Guest planner data stays separately on your device until you erase guest data in Profile or uninstall the App.
  • Account and cloud data are kept while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, we delete its sign-in record, cloud planner data and local account cache. Separately stored guest data is not deleted. A minimal security marker containing the deleted account ID and deletion-start timestamp is retained to stop older signed-in devices from recreating deleted data; it contains no planner or profile content. Copies may remain in Google's backup systems for a limited time before they are removed.
  • The erase action in guest mode removes guest courses, classes and tasks on this device; profile and preferences remain. To delete account information and its cloud backup, use Delete account while signed in.

8. Your choices and rights

You are in control of your information. Inside the App you can:

  • edit your name, university, term and grading scale in Profile;
  • edit or delete any course, class or task;
  • sign out, or use the App as a guest;
  • erase the guest planner stored on your device;
  • restore your data from the cloud backup;
  • delete your account and cloud data.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to be informed about how your data is processed, to access it, to correct it, to object to its processing, to ask for it to be erased or blocked, to receive a copy in a portable format, to withdraw consent, and to claim compensation for damages caused by unlawful processing. To use these rights, or if you need help, contact us at carlsteven0921@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with the law.

If you are in the Philippines and have a concern we cannot resolve, you may contact the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may contact your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Data sent between the App and Firebase is encrypted in transit, cloud data is protected by access rules that limit each account to its own information, and passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your phone with a screen lock, keep your password private, and sign out on shared devices.

10. Children

The App is intended for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, please contact us at carlsteven0921@gmail.com and we will delete it. Users under the age of majority should use the App with the permission of a parent or guardian.

11. Device permissions

The App asks for the minimum access it needs:

  • Internet access, to sign in, back up and sync your planner, and send provider technical diagnostics and usage data.
  • Network state, to understand whether you are online.

For registration import, Android grants access to the individual PDF or image that you select in its file picker. The App does not request broad storage access or access to your entire photo library. The bundled text recognizer can process a file already available on your device without an internet connection; choosing a file from a cloud provider may require that provider to download it first.

The App asks for permission to show notifications only when you turn on phone reminders. It does not use remote push notifications. It also asks Android to restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts, which is why it holds the "receive boot completed" permission.

12. International transfers

Our service providers operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including Singapore and the United States. We choose providers that apply appropriate safeguards for transferring and protecting personal information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add features. We will update the "Last updated" date and, for important changes, tell you in the App. Please review the policy occasionally.

14. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about your information, contact Carl at carlsteven0921@gmail.com.
