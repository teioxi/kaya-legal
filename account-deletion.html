Delete your Kaya account

Kaya is provided by Carl. You can delete your account inside the app or request help here without reinstalling it.

Delete inside the app

Sign in to the account you want to remove, open Profile → Delete account, and follow the confirmation and sign-in verification steps.

Request deletion by email

Email carlsteven0921@gmail.com from the address associated with your Kaya account. Use the subject “Kaya account deletion” and say that you want your account and related planner data deleted.

Request account deletion

Never include your password, verification code or registration document. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing a request. If you no longer have access to the account email, explain this in your request.

What is removed

Deletion removes your Firebase sign-in account, cloud student profile, courses, class schedule and tasks. In-app deletion also removes that account’s local planner cache. Separately stored guest data is kept; erase it from Profile while using guest mode.

After an email request, a disconnected device can still contain an old local copy until it reconnects or you remove the app’s local data. Deleting the app alone does not delete the cloud account. Provider backups may retain copies for a limited period under their retention policies.

For privacy questions or to follow up on a request, contact carlsteven0921@gmail.com.
